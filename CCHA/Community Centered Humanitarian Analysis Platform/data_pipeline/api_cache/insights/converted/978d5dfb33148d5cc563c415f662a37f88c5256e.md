## IFRC Humanitarian Bulletin

Population movement | Sudan

## Health update

- Increase in Malaria cases reported in Ombada, Khartoum and Omdurman.
- An on-going Measles outbreak South Darfur, with children under five making up the majority of the patients.
- The outbreak reflects the disruption of vaccination services.
- Increase in Measles cases in El Geneina (West Darfur) with hundreds of suspected cases reported every day.
- Sudan is experiencing an ongoing cholera outbreak, with 2,900 cases and 247 deaths reported to date. The outbreak comes amid flooding across eight states, while vaccination campaigns are being rolled out in West Darfur and West Kordofan.
- A cholera outbreak in El Adiya locality (West Kordofan), with 70 cases and 16 deaths reported. The area deals with severe shortages of hospitals, medical staff, medicines and other essential supplies.

## Security

- Increased insecurity in Um Baru locality (North Darfur) has displaced more than 1,200 people, with affected individuals seeking refuge in Kernoi locality and across the border in Chad.
- Escalating conflict in Blue Nile State, marked by troop mobilization and a series of drone strikes, is heightening cross-border tensions. Ethiopia has been accused of providing military assistance to parties involved in the conflict, alongside reports of increased cross-border movement.

## El Nino Impact

- Widespread rainfall deficits and unusually dry conditions across Sudan have contributed to crop failures, raising concerns over further pressure on grain prices and worsening food insecurity.
- Farmers are reportedly selling livestock as grazing land and pasture become increasingly scarce. Widespread livestock deaths have also been reported across Greater Kordofan and Greater Darfur, further undermining household livelihoods and food security.
- Population movements are being driven by worsening food insecurity and malnutrition. Since early September, more than 2,300 newly displaced people have reportedly arrived in Tawila, North Darfur, as conflict and severe food shortages continue to force civilians from their homes
- 1 · Several localities across West Kordofan and Darfur are reportedly on the brink of famine with hunger-related deaths already being reported in Jebel Marra.
- Drought-driven shortages of food and livelihoods are reportedly contributing to increased looting as communities struggle to access essential resources.

## IFRC Humanitarian Bulletin

Population movement | Neighbouring countries

## Health update

- Ongoing Cholera outbreak in Chad; latest reports from the Ministry of Health of Chad (8-11 September) show 960 cumulative cases and 45 cumulative deaths across 3 provinces (N'Djamena, Hadjer-Lamis and Lac)
- Cholera transmission remains ongoing in South Sudan, although cases are showing a declining trend. A total of 69 cases were reported between 31 August and 6 September.
- The ongoing mpox outbreak in South Sudan has reached 131 cumulative reported cases.
- More than 53,000 malaria cases have been reported across South Sudan. Despite the high caseload, this represents an approximately 40% decrease compared with the same period in 2025.

## Security

- The UN Commission on Human Rights in South Sudan has warned that the upcoming elections face serious challenges due to insecurity, inadequate funding and the risk of excluding some communities from the political process.
- The president of South Sudan replaced the head of the election commission, only about three months before the first national election since independence in 2011.
- The UN Commission on Human Rights in South Sudan has warned that the country's planned December elections could trigger further political confrontation and armed violence
- 2 · Tensions are escalating in Ethiopia's Tigray region following a series of reported drone strikes that killed three people and injured four others. The strikes have also reportedly damaged public infrastructure, including schools.
- On 12 September, a drone strike was reported near Adikong in Sudan, less than one kilometre from Adré. The recent increase in drone attacks near the border is raising concerns over the safety and continuity of humanitarian operations, with potential implications for the predictability of aid activities in the area.

## Climate related hazards

- Chad; high risk of drought with El Niño-related rainfall deficits reducing agricultural production, particularly in central and southern areas. Rising market prices (213%) are further limiting food access and worsening food insecurity among vulnerable households.
- Ethiopia; high risk of drought, heatwaves, and flash floods. El Niño-driven dry conditions are worsening food insecurity in southern and western district, while temperatures reaching 48°C in Afar and Amhara are increasing health risks. Meanwhile, Ethiopia remains at a high risk of flash flooding in western regions.
- South Sudan; high risk of drought with prolonged dry spells and El Niño conditions threatening crop growth and worsening food insecurity. With over 7.8 million people already facing acute food insecurity (IPC Phase 3 or above), drought is likely to further increase humanitarian needs.
- Uganda; high risk of drought with localized agricultural stress, particularly in Karamoja and the northern and western areas. These conditions are contributing to food insecurity, with an estimated 1.0-2.49 million people facing Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse.
